**App name:** PACE: Gentle Routine Planner
**Short description (80 chars max):**
Get out the door on time. A calm planner that never punishes a bad day.

**Full description:**

Most planners give up on you when you fall behind. PACE adjusts instead.

PACE is a calm routine app for students who keep falling off other systems. Tell it when
class starts and how long the trip takes, and it plans your mornings backwards: when to wake
up, when to eat, when to leave.

• Leave-by planner: always know when to leave, worked out from your first class.
• Snap your schedule: take a photo of your class schedule and review it before saving.
• Running late? Today shows a new plan: what to skip and when you'll arrive.
• Deadline reminders that get a little louder as the due date gets closer.
• "Just start" timer for the thing you keep putting off.
• Evening check-in: look at tomorrow, pick your top 3, go to bed.
• Low-energy mode for hard days. Light days after a rough evening.
• Tuck the turtle keeps you company and is never disappointed in you.

No streaks to lose. No red failure screens. Missing a day is fine; you just pick up again.

Private by design: no account, and everything stays on your phone.

PACE+ (₱49/month, or yearly or lifetime) adds the app gate, extra home-screen widget layouts,
budgeting and deeper trends. The core routine is always free.
