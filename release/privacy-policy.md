# PACE privacy policy

_Draft, 2026-11-19. Review before publishing, and host it at a public URL for the Play listing._

PACE (Progress Always Comes Eventually) is a routine and reminder app. This policy explains
what happens to your information.

## Your data stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays in a database on your device: your schedule, wake and sleep
times, tasks, notes, expenses, evening mood answers and app gate choices. PACE has no
account and no server. We do not collect, sell or share your information, and we cannot
see it.

## Permissions PACE asks for

- **Notifications and exact alarms:** to remind you at the right minute.
- **Run in the background (battery optimisation):** so reminders still arrive when the phone
  saves power.
- **Usage access and display over other apps (optional):** only if you turn on the app gate,
  to notice when a chosen app opens during a lock window and show the gate screen. PACE does
  not record or send which apps you use.
- **Camera roll / photos (optional):** only when you import a photo of your class schedule.
  The text is read on your device, and the photo is not kept or uploaded.

## Purchases

If you buy PACE+, Google Play handles the payment. PACE only receives confirmation that
the purchase is active. We never see your payment details.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling PACE deletes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else.

## Children

PACE is made for students. It collects no personal information from anyone, including
children.
