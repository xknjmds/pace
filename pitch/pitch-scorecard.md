## Fixes for scores under 8
- **Validation (7) → 9:** run the week-1 survey before the pitch, even with 20–30 classmates. Replace "planned" on slide 7 with one real number (sample, period, metric). This lifts Market and ROI too.
- **Market (7):** give "our classmates" a number, e.g. "about [N] students in our year level".
- **Business model / ROI (7):** add the pre-order question to the survey ("Would you pay ₱49/month for sync and backup?"); even a rough % helps.
- **Team (6):** if allowed, add one line per member of what they did, or keep the team-level skills line and let each person say it aloud.

## Delivery tips (from the guide)
- **Signpost** every handoff; the notes give each presenter their exact line.
- **Speak plainly:** explain "ROI" and "break-even" in one phrase the first time.
- **Pause** after the hook, after "₱4,500", and after "each one could prove us wrong".
- **Rehearse to time:** 4:45 planned. If you run over, cut words, don't rush.
- **Handoffs:** seven speakers means six handoffs; practise them, they're where time leaks.
- **Close** with the decision, then stop and invite questions.
