# Why now / market (raw)

## Market size (bottom-up, honest)
- **269 million** students in higher education worldwide in 2024 (UNESCO Higher Education Global Trends Report, 2026). **[Data]**
  https://thepienews.com/global-he-enrolment-reaches-269-million-as-student-mobility-triples/ · https://www.thestar.com.my/news/world/2026/05/13/higher-education-students-double-in-20-years-but-inequalities-persist-unesco
- Android is **~72.8%** of mobile OS usage worldwide (StatCounter, Nov 2025, via Backlinko / Search Engine Land). **[Data, secondary]**
  https://backlinko.com/iphone-vs-android-statistics · https://searchengineland.com/guide/iphone-vs-android-statistics
- → ~**196 million** higher-ed students likely on Android (269M × 72.8%). **[Estimate]** Assumes students match global OS share. In fact this varies a lot by country.
- About **75%** of college students call themselves procrastinators (Steel & Ferrari 2013, cited in later reviews). **[Data]** We avoid the often-quoted "80–95%", which traces back to a 1977 self-help book.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8766341 · https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.719425/full
- Excluded: high-school students (no clean global figure found). That's upside, not counted.

## Burnout / stress
- Chegg Global Student Survey 2023: **46%** of 18–21-year-olds reported academic exhaustion. **[Data, secondary snippet]** Verify against the full report before quoting on stage.
  https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/consumer-services/statistics/6875b3310e4e5/global-mental-health-challenges-faced-by-students-in-2024
- Aggregator claims (e.g. "55% of students burned out") have no clear primary source. **Not used.**

## Streaks backfire
- University of Delaware (Lerner) and the INSEAD summary: people with a broken streak were less likely to persist when the break was highlighted. Self-blame amplified this; a chance to repair weakened it. **[Data]**
  https://knowledge.insead.edu/marketing/consumer-streaks-are-motivating-the-key-is-keeping-them-alive-19011
- "63% more likely to quit" type figures: untraceable. **Not used.**

## Why now
- Calm, kind planners are winning: Tiimo was iPhone App of the Year 2025 with $4.8M raised. **[Data, secondary]**
- Android's exact-alarm rules (Android 12+) make reliable timed nudges a real engineering problem. PACE has already solved the permission flow (see `NOTES.md`). Real-device verification is still pending, so state it honestly.
- Timing strength: **moderate**. The main "why now" is personal (a real problem the founder has today) plus proof from comparable apps that the market wants this. Don't oversell it.
