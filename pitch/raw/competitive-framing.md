## Likely judge objections
- "Isn't this just a calendar or Todoist?" → Those store *what* and *when it's due*. PACE computes *when to start*, and rebuilds the plan when the morning slips.
- "Tiimo already exists and won App of the Year." → Good: it proves people want calm planners. Tiimo is a canvas you fill in; PACE fills it in for you from one input (class time), and it works on Android with no account.
- "Why would anyone stick with it without streaks?" → Research on broken streaks (University of Delaware / INSEAD) shows a highlighted broken streak lowers persistence, and self-blame makes it worse. PACE counts what got done instead.
