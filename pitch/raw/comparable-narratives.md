- Raised $4.8M total pre-Series A (company newsroom, 2025). **[Data]**

## Takeaway for PACE
There is a proven appetite for calm, kind planners (Tiimo, Finch). None of these plans the **morning backwards from class time**, and none treats **lateness as a single next step**. Our framing: "a calm planner that does the planning for you".
