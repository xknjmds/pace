## Fixes for scores under 7
- **Market (6):** add one local, bottom-up line on slide 10: "[N] students at [Our school]; we'll start with [N] classmates."
- **Business model (5):** keep it short and honest. If a judge pushes, the answer is "zero running costs today, so there's no pressure to monetise before the pilot proves value."
- **Ask (6):** add one sentence to slide 13: "We're looking for [pilot partners / mentoring on X]."
- **Sequencing (7):** optionally say "it's already built and tested" in slide 2.

## Delivery tips
- **The 60-second rule:** the judges decide early. Rehearse slides 1–3 until they're effortless.
- **Invite conversation:** the question on slide 3 is deliberate. If a judge starts talking, let them.
- **Own the numbers:** know 269M → 196M → 147M and where each comes from (appendix table).
- **"I don't know" is fine:** "We haven't measured that yet; the pilot will tell us" beats a guess.
- **Demo insurance:** record a 60-second screen capture of the demo path in case the phone or projector fails.
- **Practise the opener** until it's automatic: "PACE tells you what to do right now."
