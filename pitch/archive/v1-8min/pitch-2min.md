# 2-minute verbal pitch: PACE

*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 2026-11-19*

Word-for-word, about 290 words. No slides needed. **Bold** = stress. `/` = short pause. `//` = full pause.

> Hi, we're [Team name]. // We built **PACE**, a calm Android app that tells a student **what to do right now.** //
>
> Here's how. You tell it when your first class starts / and how long your commute is. PACE works **backwards**: up at six-thirty, breakfast at six-fifty, out the door at seven thirty-five. / Each step rings at the right time. //
>
> We built it because one of us had **no daily system at all.** Late to class, breakfast skipped, assignments remembered the night they were due / and recovering from burnout on top of that. / And we're not alone: about **three in four** college students call themselves procrastinators. //
>
> Here's what we learned. / To-do apps store the due date, but they never tell you **when to start.** And habit apps reset your streak to zero the first time you slip / which makes tired people quit. //
>
> So our insight is simple: **people who are behind don't need more lists. They need one nudge at the right moment, and forgiveness when they miss it.** //
>
> That's PACE. If you oversleep, it doesn't go red and show you everything you missed. It shows **one card**: "Leave now. You arrive about eight-oh-five." / There's a low-energy mode for bad days, a ten-minute "just start" timer for homework, and no streaks, ever. //
>
> And it's **real.** It's built in Flutter with 335 automated tests, it works offline with no account, and your data never leaves your phone. //
>
> Next, we're piloting it with students at [Our school]. / PACE stands for *Progress Always Comes Eventually.* / Thank you, and we'd love to show you the app.

## Delivery notes
- Say the opening two sentences slowly; they carry the whole pitch.
- Smile on "no streaks, ever".
- If you're cut at 60 seconds, stop after the insight paragraph.

## Red flags
- No users yet; don't imply otherwise.

## Yellow flags
- "Three in four" is a self-report figure (Steel & Ferrari 2013).
