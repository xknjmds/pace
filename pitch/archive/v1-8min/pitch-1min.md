# 1-minute elevator pitch: PACE

*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 2026-11-19*

## Formal (judges, competition floor), about 140 words
> We're [Team name], and we built PACE: a calm Android app that tells a student what to do right now.
> You enter when class starts and how long the commute is, and PACE works backwards: when to wake, eat and leave, each with a reminder. If you oversleep, it doesn't list what you missed; it shows one step: "Leave now. You arrive about 08:05."
> Our insight: people who are behind don't need more lists. They need one nudge at the right moment, and forgiveness when they miss it. So PACE has no streaks, no red, and a low-energy mode for bad days.
> It's built and tested, works offline with no account, and we're piloting it at [Our school] next. We'd love to show you.

## Casual (hallway, networking), about 80 words
> You know how to-do apps tell you something's due, but never when to start? We built PACE. You tell it when class starts, and it tells you when to wake up, eat and leave. If you're late, it just says "leave now", no guilt trip. Our turtle, Tuck, is never disappointed in you. It's built; want to see it?
