# Note to organisers and judges: PACE

*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 2026-11-19*

The skill's "investor cold email", adapted for a student competition: an entry summary, a short note to send to judges or mentors before the event, and a follow-up.

## Subject line options
1. PACE: an app that tells students what to do right now ([Team name])
2. [Team name] entry: a calm planner that plans the morning for you
3. Built, tested, and never guilt-trips you: PACE by [Team name]

## Entry summary / pre-event note (~230 words)
> Dear [Judge / Organiser name],
>
> We're [Team name] from [Our school], and our entry is **PACE**, a calm Android app that tells a student what to do right now.
>
> **The problem.** One of us had no daily system: late to class, breakfast skipped, submissions remembered too late, while recovering from burnout. About three in four college students call themselves procrastinators. To-do apps store due dates but never say when to start, and habit apps reset streaks to zero, which makes tired people give up.
>
> **What PACE does.** Enter when class starts and how long the commute is, and PACE works backwards to when to wake, eat and leave, with a reminder for each. If the morning slips, Today shows one next step ("Leave now. You arrive about 08:05.") instead of a list of misses. Deadlines get four gentle reminders, there's a ten-minute "just start" timer, and a low-energy mode for bad days. No streaks, no red.
>
> **Where we are.** Built in Flutter: 13 feature modules and 335 automated tests. It works offline with no account. We're honest that it has no users beyond us yet; a pilot at [Our school] is next.
>
> We'd love your feedback, and we'll bring a live demo.
>
> Thank you,
> [Member names]

## Personalising it
- **Teacher / academic judge:** lead with the burnout and procrastination problem, and the forgiving design.
- **Industry / tech judge:** lead with "built and tested" (pure-Dart scheduling logic, 335 tests, offline-first, Android exact-alarm handling).
- **Business / entrepreneurship judge:** lead with the market funnel and the free-core, paid-sync path (marked as untested).

## Red flags
- Don't send the follow-up's "since then" line with a made-up number; leave it out if nothing has changed.

## Yellow flags
- Replace every [bracket] before sending.
