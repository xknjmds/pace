# 2-minute verbal pitch: PACE

*Skill: startup-pitch | Generated: 2026-11-19 (rebuilt to follow the Topic 08 guide)*

About 300 words, no slides needed. It keeps the guide's arc: **hook → solution → business model → proof + ROI → specific ask**. **Bold** = stress. `/` = short pause. `//` = full pause.

> **0:00 · Hook.** 7:41 AM. Class starts at 8:00. / Baby just woke up. // She skips breakfast, runs, and walks in at 8:15 / the quiz has already started. And the lab report due at 9? She remembered it at 11 last night. // Baby is made up, but that morning is ours. And about **three in four** college students call themselves procrastinators. //
>
> **0:25 · Solution.** We're main.dart, and we built **PACE**. You enter your class time once, and PACE works out when to **wake, eat and leave**, with a reminder at each step. If you're late, it shows **one** next step: "Leave now." / Deadlines get four reminders that come closer, and there's a low-energy mode for bad days. **No streaks, no red.** // It's already built: 335 automated tests, offline, no account. //
>
> **1:00 · Business model.** The planner is free. Students pay **₱49 a month** for Plus, which keeps their plan synced and backed up across phone and laptop. //
>
> **1:15 · Proof + ROI.** We don't have customer data yet, so we'll test it: a survey for our baseline, a set-up test, then an 8-week pilot with our 30 classmates measuring whether students stay, arrive late less often, and pre-order Plus. / And the price is small: Plus pays for itself if PACE saves just **1.4 minutes a school day**. That's a model, and we'll measure the real number. //
>
> **1:45 · Ask.** We're asking for **₱4,500 to run an 8-week pilot with our 30 classmates**. / By week 10, we'll report who stayed, whether they're late less often, and who would pay. // Approve and fund the pilot in our classroom. / Thank you.

## Delivery notes
- Pause after the hook and after "₱4,500".
- If you're cut short, drop the business model sentence, never the ask.

## Red flags
- No users yet; don't imply otherwise.

## Yellow flags
- "1.4 minutes a day" is a break-even from a model (₱49 ÷ ₱94/h ÷ 22 days), not a measured result.
