## Summary (~230 words)
> Dear [Professor / Panel],
>
> We're main.dart, and our startup is **PACE**, an Android app that helps students who keep running late start the right thing at the right time.
>
> **Problem.** A student wakes at 7:41 for an 8:00 class, skips breakfast, walks in 15 minutes into a quiz, and remembers a deadline the night before. About three in four college students call themselves procrastinators. To-do apps store due dates but never say when to start.
>
> **Approach.** Students enter their class time once; PACE works out when to wake, eat and leave, reminds them at each step, and shows one next step when they're late. Deadlines get four reminders; low-energy mode and no streaks keep it forgiving. The app is built and tested (335 automated tests, offline, no account).
>
> **Business model.** The planner is free; students pay ₱49/month for Plus (sync and backup). Our model says Plus pays for itself if PACE saves 1.4 minutes a school day; we will measure the real figure.
>
> **Evidence.** We have no customer data yet. Our plan: a baseline survey (week 1), a set-up test (week 2), and an 8-week pilot with our 30 classmates (weeks 3–10).
>
> **Next step.** We ask for **₱4,500 for an 8-week pilot with our 30 classmates**: ₱50 of load each as thanks, one onboarding session, and the one-time Play Store fee.
