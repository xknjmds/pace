## Formal (judges, panel), about 150 words
> 7:41 AM, class at 8:00, and Baby just woke up. She runs in late, misses the start of a quiz, and remembers her lab report the night before. Three in four college students call themselves procrastinators.
> We're main.dart, and we built PACE. You enter your class time once, and it works out when to wake, eat and leave, with a reminder at each step. Late? It shows one next step: "Leave now." No streaks, no guilt.
> The planner is free; students pay ₱49 a month for sync and backup. It's built and tested, but we don't have customer data yet.
