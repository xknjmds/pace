# Storylines (picked: A)

**A. One morning (picked).** The day as a clock. 06:30, Tuck sleeps in, the minutes slip to 07:48,
then PACE shows one next thing ("Leave now · you still arrive on time"). Wake/leave timeline,
deadlines, just-start focus, low-energy evening, cairn end card.

**B. Stones stack.** Match cuts on the cairn: each stone is one beat (on time / just start / rest
counts); a round shape holds across every cut (stone → focus ring → timeline dot → stone).

**C. Slow is fast.** Loud kinetic type tries to rush ("HURRY", "DON'T BREAK THE STREAK") while Tuck
walks through unbothered; then calm, and the real screens pass by like scenery along Tuck's walk.
